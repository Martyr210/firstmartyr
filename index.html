About us

Where little ones begin.

First Martyr is a caring daycare and pre-primary school at Nanenane, inside the Maonyesho Grounds in Morogoro. From Daycare to KG III, every child has a safe and loving place to play, learn and grow, and starts primary school ready and confident.
